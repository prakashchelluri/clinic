What KiviHealth and PatientNow have that we don't

KiviHealth is the cheap, broad Indian clinic suite. PatientNow is the US aesthetics platform built around clinical photos and treatment series. On the front office we already match or beat KiviHealth. On the procedure side, which is where a dermatology clinic earns its money, PatientNow is far ahead of us. That is where we should build next.

Recommendation

What to build next, in order

Ranked by what a busy dermatologist would use every day, and by how far each item puts us ahead of KiviHealth at a similar price.

  1. Procedure photo timeline

    Take photos on the phone during a sitting, tagged by area (face, scalp, back). See each area's photos across sittings side by side, with an overlay of the last photo to line up the shot.

    The single biggest gap. PatientNow sells this as an add-on. KiviHealth only stores files.

  2. Treatment course view

    “Laser hair reduction, sitting 3 of 8” with each sitting's device settings (fluence, spot size, passes), photos and notes, and the next sitting suggested at the right gap.

    Uses the procedure courses and room scheduling we already have.

  3. Consent signed on the phone

    One consent template per procedure, signed on the clinic phone or tablet, stored with the sitting. Photo consent controls whether a photo may ever be shared.

  4. Automatic recall

    WhatsApp messages when a follow-up or the next sitting is due, and for patients who stopped coming. These replace reception's two calls per procedure patient. The message outbox from the booking work is already in place.

    Needs the clinic's WhatsApp Business account connected.

  5. Prepaid sitting packages

    Sell 6 sittings up front and draw one down at each visit, using the existing charges and payments ledger.

  6. KiviHealth parity

    Smaller items Indian clinics expect:

    • GST invoice PDF
    • Share prescriptions on WhatsApp
    • Excel export of reports
    • ABHA / ABDM, later
